THE PHOTO COLLECTION

Can the archive
become a game?

This prototype uses existing photographs with source records. It asks for a month and day, never a year. “Where” asks for the main landmark shown. Map rounds use mapped landmark coordinates or pins you approve in the review desk. Distances are approximate: source coordinates and the basemap are not surveyed ground truth.

Photos with only a posting date are excluded. Capture dates come from explicit Garden captions, camera metadata, or photographer-supplied “taken on” dates.

This switches to the Creative Commons collection. Attribution and license links appear with each answer. Camera dates are documented source claims, not independently audited timestamps.

Images remain credited to their photographers. Public availability does not establish permission for a public game. This is an independent feasibility prototype, not an official Garden product.
