A NOTE BEFORE I GO

Six months.
So much to be grateful for.

After six months at the Garden, I wanted to leave a little something to enjoy together. This game is a thank-you for the time we’ve shared and the care you bring to this place.

A kind welcome. A patient answer. Helping someone feel at ease. The small things you do in Visitor Relations matter, even when you don’t get to see how much.

As I move on, I’ll carry a lot of appreciation for this team. I hope you keep finding moments to enjoy the Garden yourselves, and keep looking after one another.

Thank you for being part of my six months here. I’ll miss you. Stay curious, be kind to yourselves, and save a little time for another round.

With gratitude, and a very fond goodbye.
